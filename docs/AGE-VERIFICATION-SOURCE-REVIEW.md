# Age-verification source review

Reviewed: 25 July 2026

The customer guidance and AI knowledge were reviewed against official UK sources current on the review date:

- Ofcom guidance on highly effective age assurance and methods capable or not capable of meeting that standard;
- Ofcom's 2026 report on the use of age assurance;
- the Online Safety Act 2023 definition confirming that self-declaration alone does not count as age verification or age estimation for the Act;
- ICO age-assurance guidance for the Children’s Code, including risk assessment, proportionality, data minimisation and the limitations of self-declaration.

This review records the source basis only. It is not a substitute for the company’s DPIA, Children’s Code assessment, regulated-service scoping, provider due diligence or legal advice.
