# Planyx age-verification customer guidance

Last reviewed: 25 July 2026

## Customer account rule

Planyx customer accounts are available only to people aged 16 or over. Customers aged 16–17 receive mandatory enhanced privacy and safeguarding defaults. People under 16 may view public information but must not register for or use a customer account.

## Current Planyx method

The current date-of-birth form is a signed self-declaration used to decide Planyx account eligibility and apply the correct age band. It is not independent proof of age and must not be described as Ofcom highly effective age assurance.

Self-declaration may be proportionate for a lower-risk service only where supported by a documented assessment. It cannot be relied on as a standalone highly effective method where the Online Safety Act requires age verification or age estimation, or where the service presents higher risks to children.

## Stronger methods

Where a stronger check is required, a properly assessed provider journey may use open banking, photo-ID matching, facial age estimation, a mobile-network-operator check, a credit-card check for an 18+ threshold, email-based age estimation or a digital identity service.

The full implementation must be technically accurate, robust, reliable and fair. Planyx should receive only the minimum result needed, such as an age threshold, age band, reference and expiry.

## Privacy and safe handling

Customers must enter a date of birth only in the secure age-check field. They must not place dates of birth, passports, driving licences, selfies, bank information, payment details, cookies, tokens or authentication secrets into the AI guide or an ordinary enquiry.

The current Planyx process encrypts the date of birth in a restricted age-verification record. It is masked by default and access is audited. The ordinary customer profile uses only eligibility, age band and safeguarding status.

## Customer support

The Age Verification Guide may explain the process, privacy, safeguards, stronger methods, maintenance and review options. It cannot estimate, approve, reject or override anyone’s age. It must not provide instructions to bypass or falsify the check.

## Governance sources

The control framework should be reviewed against current Ofcom highly effective age-assurance guidance, the Online Safety Act 2023, ICO age-assurance and Children’s Code guidance, the Planyx DPIA, provider due diligence and the applicable privacy notices.
